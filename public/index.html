How to Play
The Chain

Each day there are 4 hidden words linked in a chain. Every word connects to the one before it. A theme clue at the top hints at the chain's nature — it's cryptic, but it will make sense once you solve it.

Each round reveals a clue that explicitly names the previous word and nudges you toward the next one.

Guessing

Type any 5-letter word and press Enter. You have 6 guesses in total across all 4 rounds — every wrong guess counts toward that limit, so use them wisely.

After each guess the tiles reveal how close you were:

Daily Streak & Rewards

Play every day to build your streak. Hit a milestone and unlock a one-time reward for that day's puzzle. Rewards reset after 30 days — use them or lose them.
